Scope

One CIDR per line. Nothing outside these ranges can be scanned, pinged, or contacted — this is the boundary the whole application enforces, not a default that can be overridden per action. Private ranges only (10/8, 172.16/12, 192.168/16, 100.64/10).

Rejects any range larger than this. 1024 is a /22. Keeps a mistyped prefix from becoming a scan of sixteen million addresses.
